# Exp29: executable target-bridge audit

This panel independently recomputes the finite contiguous-start enumeration used by the common witness and quantifies the residual between the aggregate risk contract and the resulting job-indexed profile. It records the candidate count, objective recomputation residual, target-tracking residuals on total and flexible scales, and regional capacity utilization. The audit uses the locked declaration witness only; future arrivals and execution telemetry are excluded.

The contract-to-profile residual is retained as a measured certificate. The panel does not impose a post-solve profile correction or a heuristic target-fitting step.
